# Colony progression

The stages form a hierarchy of needs: work on the lowest stage with an unmet item, and move up only once it is complete.

## Stage 1: Survive
- Every colonist has a bed in a roofed, enclosed room.
- Food runway is at least 5 days and a crop is in the ground (food-security).
- A roofed stockpile near the base for food, medicine and materials.
- A stove or cooking spot with a meal bill.
- A research project running that pays off on this map now (better crops, batteries, a power source).

## Stage 2: Stabilize
- A freezer, a dining room with a table and chairs, one recreation item.
- A generator plus batteries; lights in the main rooms.
- A research bench and a crafting bench in steady use.
- A defensive position: walls, chokepoints, cover.
- Hospital beds next to the medicine stockpile.

## Stage 3: Grow
- Individual bedrooms, floors, a recreation room.
- Production chains: components, weapons and armor, medicine when research allows.
- Recruit in step with stages 1 and 2: each new colonist needs a bed and runway too.
- Trade surplus for what the map lacks (components, steel, medicine).

## Stage 4: Refine
- Stone walls around living areas for fire safety.
- Bionics, advanced research, a stronger killbox, backup power.
- The player's long-term goal, if they have one.

## Wealth and raids
Raid strength scales with colony wealth. Grow defenses and fighters at the same pace as wealth.

## Pick one action now
1. Safety first: runway under 5 days, no doctor, missing beds, dangerous temperatures.
2. Otherwise the cheapest item that completes the current stage.
3. Prefer compounding actions (a research project, a production bill, a crop field) over one-off tidying.
4. Confirm materials exist and a colonist has the needed work enabled before placing blueprints or bills.
Done when one action from the current stage is started and you can name the stage and what comes after it.
